An industry-backed “permitting bill” approved by the US Senate would gut key clean water protections with changes that would do little to speed up the development of major energy projects, advocates warn.

The Bipartisan American Affordability and Jobs Act of 2026 (Baaja) passed the Senate 62-33 and is now being debated in the House. The Baaja is a “permitting reform” bill that aims to reduce the time it takes federal agencies to approve major energy and infrastructure projects, like power lines, fossil fuel pipelines, natural gas plants and datacenters.
