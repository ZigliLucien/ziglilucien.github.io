Hannah Lillith Assadi wins US National book award for ‘visionary’ novel Paradiso 17
back

Assadi’s third novel was inspired by the story of her late father, a Palestinian who escaped the Nakba as a child in 1948

Palestinian-Jewish author Hannah Lillith Assadi has won the National book award for fiction, one of the US’s most prestigious literary prizes, for her novel Paradiso 17.

Assadi’s third novel is a study of the psychic toll of displacement on migrants, through the tale of a Palestinian man who escaped the Nakba in 1948, when more than 700,000 Palestinians fled or were expelled from their homes by Israel. The story was inspired by her father, Sami Abdul-Fattah Al-Assadi, who fled the Nakba as a child with his family, before studying in Italy and moving to New York.

back

Storm expected to move into southern California, with LA residents warned of potential evacuation orders

Heavy rain pounded California’s Bay area, with record rainfall in downtown San Francisco and flooding and power outages across the region, as an “atmospheric river” arrived on Wednesday and dumped precipitation across the state.

The storm was expected to move into southern California on Wednesday night and Thursday, with Los Angeles warning residents in mudslide-prone areas of potential evacuation orders to come. Southern Californians were also warned of potential coastal flooding, heavy surf, wave damage to coastal piers and parking lots and beach sand erosion. Heavy snow warnings were issued for higher elevations of the Sierra Mountains, including in the Greater Lake Tahoe area.
