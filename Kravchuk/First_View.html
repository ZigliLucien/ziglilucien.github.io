What are Krawtchouk polynomials?

  • Generalized binomial coefficients (Pascal-Krawtchouk triangle)
  • Energy functions for Twistons
  • Symmetric functions of a drunkard's walk
  • Eigenvalues of the operator A in a representation of SL(2,R)=SO(2,1)
  • Supersymmetry operator
Although easily and very intuitively defined, they have a deep meaning and reveal connections with Lie theory, Fermion-Boson field theory, signal processing, data storage, Fourier theory, trans-galactic missions, etc., etc.
