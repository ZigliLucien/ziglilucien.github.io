Twistons

This section provides some heuristics rather then really proposing a ``physical theory.'' Treat it as a ``toy theory'' that describes hypothetical particles called``twistons.'' Kravchuk polynomials may be treated as generalized potentials of interaction.

A twiston may have one of two states: regular or twisted. This can be viewed as two possible states of a ribbon closed to form a band (representing a twiston).

Note. As well, we may associate ``spin up'' and ``spin down'' respectively as a mental aid, but notice the difference: spin is interchangeable with respect to viewer position, twist is not.

Now, let a system be composed of N such particles. One may calculate the total twist of a system; this will be just the number j of twisted particles among the N.

The system state may be described by two numbers i,j.

Define the 1st ``potential'' of a system of N twistons with i untwisted and j twisted twistons as

f 1 (j,N) =i-j.

Next is the question of the potential coming from pairwise interactions. Two particles interact by opening themselves and forming a new particle by gluing.

Gluing two twisted particles cancels the twist. In other words, the potential of interaction of a pair is the product of the sum of two twists modulo 2.
