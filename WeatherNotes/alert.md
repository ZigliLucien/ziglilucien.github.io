   <h2>Winter Weather Advisory issued November 25 at 12:51AM CST until November 26 at 12:00PM CST by NWS Paducah KY</h2>
   <div style="font-size:120%">Our first winter storm of the season is set to impact portions of
      our region as we head into the Thanksgiving holiday. A sharp gradient
      between very little snow and impactful snow amounts is expected. That
      sharp cutoff appears likely to setup within 25 miles of the
      Mississippi River, with latest trends shifting further west with the
      heavier axis.
      
      * WHAT...Wet snow expected. Total snow accumulations of 1 to 2 inches
      with the highest totals potentially approaching 3 inches near the
      Mississippi River.
      
      * WHERE...Alexander, Jackson, Perry IL, and Union Counties in
      southern Illinois.
      
      * WHEN...From 3 PM this afternoon to noon CST Thursday.
      
      * IMPACTS...Plan on slippery road conditions. The hazardous
      conditions will impact the Wednesday evening and Thursday morning
      commutes.
