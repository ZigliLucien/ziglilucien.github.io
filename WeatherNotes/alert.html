Winter Weather Advisory issued November 25 at 10:28AM CST until November 26 at 12:00PM CST by NWS Paducah KY

Our first winter storm of the season is set to impact portions of our region as we head into the Thanksgiving holiday. A sharp gradient between very little snow and impactful snow amounts is expected. * WHAT...Wet snow expected. Total snow accumulations up to two inches. * WHERE...Alexander, Jackson, Perry IL, and Union Counties. * WHEN...From 3 PM this afternoon to noon CST Thursday. * IMPACTS...Plan on slippery road conditions. The hazardous conditions will impact the Wednesday evening and Thursday morning commutes.
