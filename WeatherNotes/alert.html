Special Weather Statement issued September 12 at 11:35PM CDT by NWS Paducah KY

High temperatures today will reach the lower to middle 90s. Combined with the humidity, heat index values will rise to near or just above 100 degrees this afternoon. If you have outdoor plans, prepare for impacts from the heat by staying hydrated, wearing cool, loose-fitting clothing and, taking frequent breaks in the shade or in an air-conditioned location.
