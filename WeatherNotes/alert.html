Freeze Watch issued November 6 at 12:11AM CST until November 8 at 8:00AM CST by NWS Paducah KY

* WHAT...For the Freeze Warning, sub-freezing temperatures as low as 30 expected. For the Freeze Watch, sub-freezing temperatures as low as 29 possible. * WHERE...Portions of the Ozark Foothills of southeast Missouri and south and southwest Illinois. * WHEN...For the Freeze Warning, from 11 PM this evening to 8 AM CST Saturday. For the Freeze Watch, from Saturday evening through Sunday morning. * IMPACTS...Frost and freeze conditions could kill crops, other sensitive vegetation and possibly damage unprotected outdoor plumbing. * ADDITIONAL DETAILS...The hard freezes will bring the growing season to an end.
