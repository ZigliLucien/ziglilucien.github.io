Winter Storm Watch issued November 24 at 12:34PM CST until November 26 at 12:00PM CST by NWS Paducah KY

An approaching system is showing an increased risk of producing a quick bout of heavy wet snow late Wednesday night into Thursday. This heavy snow could produce substantial impacts to travel for a period of time on Thanksgiving Day. * WHAT...Heavy wet snow possible. Total snow accumulations between 3 and 5 inches possible. * WHERE...Portions of southern Illinois and southeast Missouri. * WHEN...From Wednesday afternoon through Thursday morning. * IMPACTS...Plan on slippery road conditions. The hazardous conditions could impact the Wednesday evening and Thursday morning commutes.
